A Time for Family and Blessings

Dashain is one of Nepal's most beloved festivals. Families come together to receive tika and jamara, seek blessings from elders, fly kites, and enjoy precious moments with their loved ones.

🪔 🌼 ✨

A Celebration of Light and Happiness

Tihar is a beautiful five-day festival celebrated with lights, flowers, rangoli, and worship. People honor crows, dogs, cows, and Goddess Laxmi, share gifts, and celebrate Bhai Tika with love between brothers and sisters.
